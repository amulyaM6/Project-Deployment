COVERAGE NOTE (important — read before using):
NFHS-5 covered India in two phases. Phase-I (22 states/UTs) data is public and free.
Phase-II covered the remaining 14 states/UTs but was delayed by COVID-19; a single
combined free compendium for all 36 states/UTs was not located as of this data pull
(Sept 2026) — it may exist behind institutional/paid access, or under a different
document name worth searching for separately.
Within Phase-I itself, only 5 of the 22 states/UTs are captured below in full detail;
the remaining 17 Phase-I states have factsheets in the same source PDF but were not
extracted in this pass due to length. This file should be treated as a partial,
in-progress health dataset, not a complete one — expand it before relying on it for
anything beyond these 5 states.
