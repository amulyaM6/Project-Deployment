WHAT THIS SCORE MEANS:
A single 0-100 composite score per state, built from 24 indicators across three domains:
Health Outcomes (mortality rates, sex ratio at birth — highest weight), Governance and
Information, and Key Inputs and Processes (staffing, facility functionality, etc). Higher =
better health system performance. This is a DIFFERENT kind of number from the NFHS file
(which has 131 raw survey indicators) — this is a single ready-made composite score, useful
as a quick per-state health baseline.

COVERAGE: 19 of 19 "Larger States" (full coverage), 3 of 8 "Smaller States", 3 of 7 Union
Territories. West Bengal did not participate in this round (no score available from any
edition after this). Ladakh has no data (created as a UT after this survey's cutoff).
Several Smaller States and UTs are not included below because their exact scores could not
be confidently read from the source PDF's chart data on this pass — see NOTES.
